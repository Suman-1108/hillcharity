OUR PROJECTS

  • Home
  • OUR PROJECTS

Women Empowerment

With a greater vision to be a change in the lives of many women and children, women empowerment programs are held in rural villages to bring out the skills in them to become a challenging entrepreneurs. Vocational trainings are given for rural women and women are also encouraged to continue their education.

Child Development Centres

Child development centres are opened in villages to help rural kids to develop themselves in education, sports, skill development. Evening tuition classes are taken for the below poverty line children to help them in their education followed by a healthy meal. These kids are provided with educational kits, arts training, spoken English training and counselling.
Each child development centre concentrates on each child’s development travelling along with them through schooling and college supporting each kid’s uniqueness and helping them to be a successful person and as an inspiration to many in the development of the society.

Abandoned Children Home

Girl children who are abandoned by their parents and relatives are given shelter in hope children home. Children are taken care as they would be in their family. Nutritious food, education, sports training, medical care.

Home for Aged

Elderly women who are abandoned by their children, homeless, left in the streets to survive on their own are rescued from the streets and are given shelter, food, education and medical care with care and affection.

Awareness Programs

Awareness on girl child education, child abuse, prevention of child marriage, women physical and mental health. Awareness programs are conducted in schools, colleges and rural areas of tamilnadu.

Disaster Relief Program

Through our disaster relief program we help and support areas affected by natural calamities. Relief supplies are distributed to people through volunteers.

Feed the Hungry

Fth program receives excess food from marriage halls, hotels and distributes them to poor, hungry people in and around the areas of tuticorin and Tirunelveli.
